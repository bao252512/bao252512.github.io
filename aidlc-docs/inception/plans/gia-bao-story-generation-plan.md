# Gia Bao story-generation plan

## Context and proposed approach

Requirements: ../requirements/gia-bao-requirements.md. Use a short journey-based set of stories, each with a persona, benefit, and observable acceptance criteria. Proposed journeys: understand Gia Bao's focus; evaluate the two projects; review experience and skills; inspect evidence; download the resume and use the site on mobile/keyboard.

## Breakdown alternatives

- Journey-based (recommended): follows a reviewer from introduction through project evaluation to supporting evidence; best fit for a small personal portfolio.
- Feature-based: one story per section; easy coverage, but can repeat reviewer goals.
- Persona-based: group by admissions/recruiter/owner; makes audience needs explicit, but duplicates shared browsing behavior.
- Domain-based: group by education, projects, and evidence; precise content coverage, less emphasis on visitor progression.
- Epic-based: larger portfolio epic with smaller stories; adds unnecessary hierarchy at this scope.
